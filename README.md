# Zoomies 🚗

**Making student travel easier, smarter, and more connected.**

## About

Zoomies is a student-focused carpooling and travel coordination platform designed to help college students find people travelling to the same destination.

Whether a student has a vehicle and wants to offer a ride or is looking for someone to travel with, Zoomies makes it easier to connect with fellow students, coordinate trips, and share travel expenses.

## Key Features

- **Offer a Ride:** Students can list available seats in their personal vehicles or shared cabs.
- **Find a Ride:** Discover other students travelling to the same destination.
- **Smart Matching:** Get suggestions based on destination, travel date, departure time, and pickup location.
- **Manual Search:** Browse available travel listings and find suitable matches.
- **Join Requests:** Request to join a ride, subject to approval.
- **Student Community:** Connect and coordinate travel with fellow college students.

## How It Works

1. Sign up using your college email.
2. Create a travel listing by offering a ride or looking for one.
3. Discover students travelling along similar routes.
4. Send or receive ride requests.
5. Connect with other students and coordinate your journey.

## Tech Stack

- React Native & Expo
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Google Maps Platform

## Project Vision

Our goal is to make everyday student travel more convenient, affordable, and collaborative by building a trusted campus-first travel community.
